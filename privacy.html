Privacy policy

How Pixel Tools handles privacy.

Effective date: May 1, 2026. This policy explains what happens when you use this website and its image tools.

Image processing

The Image to WebP converter is designed to run in your browser. Your selected image is processed locally on your device and is not intentionally uploaded to our server by the converter.

Downloaded output files are created only when you choose to export them from the tool.

Information we may collect

Like most websites, hosting providers and browser-based services may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and timestamps for security, diagnostics, and performance.

If analytics or advertising tools are enabled in the future, they may collect usage information according to their own policies and the consent settings available in your region.

Cookies and advertising

This website is prepared for Google AdSense. When live ads are enabled, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads based on visits to this and other websites.

Users can manage Google ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. Some third-party advertising partners may also offer opt-out choices through their own websites or industry opt-out tools such as AboutAds.

Third-party services

Pages may include third-party services such as Google AdSense, content delivery networks, or embedded scripts needed for tools and monetization. These services may receive technical information from your browser when their resources load.

External links

This website may link to guides, resources, or third-party websites. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external websites.

Children's privacy

Pixel Tools is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this website, contact the site owner so it can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, tools, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when meaningful changes are made.
