About

Simple image tools for everyday publishing work.

Pixel Tools is a small collection of practical browser-based utilities for people who need lighter, better-sized images without opening a heavy design app.

What we build

Our tools focus on common image tasks: converting images to WebP, compressing large image files, and resizing visuals for websites, blogs, social media, stores, and portfolios.

The goal is to make image optimization approachable. You choose a file, adjust the settings you need, and download the result.

Principles

Designed for speed, clarity, and privacy.

Image tools should be easy to understand, fast to use, and honest about what happens to your files.

Useful first

Each page starts with the actual tool, not a maze of promotions or instructions.

Browser-based

Where possible, processing happens locally in your browser so your image workflow stays quick and direct.

Accessible content

We add plain-language guides to help people understand image formats, compression, and sizing.

Advertising and support

Pixel Tools may use advertising to keep the tools free. Ad placements are clearly labeled and are kept separate from download buttons, navigation links, and tool controls.

Before enabling live ads, the website owner must replace the placeholder AdSense publisher ID and ad slot values with real account values from Google AdSense.
