Why WebP

Smaller images help pages load faster.

WebP is widely supported by modern browsers and can reduce image weight while keeping good visual quality. That means faster pages, smoother browsing, and better image performance for blogs, stores, portfolios, and social content.

Better performance

Use WebP versions of large JPG or PNG files to reduce page weight and improve perceived load speed.

Transparent images

PNG images with transparency can be exported to WebP while preserving transparent areas in supported browsers.

Simple workflow

Quick answers

Image to WebP FAQ

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This converter uses your browser's canvas tools to create the WebP file locally on your device.

What quality setting should I use?

Start around 80 to 85 percent for a good balance of detail and smaller file size. Raise it for photography and lower it for thumbnails or quick-loading previews.

Can I use the converted WebP files on my website?

Yes. Download the WebP file and upload it to your site like any other image. For best compatibility, keep original images available if your site needs older browser fallbacks.
